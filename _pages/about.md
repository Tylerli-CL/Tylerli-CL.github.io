Welcome! 👋 I am **Chengliang Li**, also known as Tyler. I am a **Robotics Engineer based in Shanghai**, where I work on robot localization, multi-sensor calibration and perception for autonomous robots. My research interests center on **spatial intelligence for robotics**, connecting **3D perception and scene understanding** with **task planning and execution**. The goal is to help robots understand the physical world and use that understanding to carry out tasks more effectively. 🤖

I received my **M.Sc. in Mechatronics and Robotics** from [Leibniz University Hannover](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/) (**Germany 🇩🇪**) in **October 2024**, specializing in Robotics and Mobile Systems. My research in Hannover included:

- **GNSS-denied LiDAR localization (master's thesis):** Research at the [Institute of Cartography and Geoinformatics (IKG)](https://www.ikg.uni-hannover.de/) explored **point-cloud-to-image conversion** for global localization, transforming 3D LiDAR point clouds into 2D bird's-eye-view images for feature matching, followed by 3D geometric refinement. The resulting framework, **HybridGLoc**, was published at **ICARA 2026**.
- **Room Graph Prediction from Point Cloud Maps:** Research at the [Real-Time Systems Group (RTS)](https://www.ise.uni-hannover.de/rts/) combined point-cloud learning and graph neural networks to model connections between indoor rooms.

Before my current role, I worked as a Computer Vision Algorithm Engineer at [Xiaomi](https://www.mi.com/global/). I also completed internships at [Huawei Intelligent Automotive Solution BU (IAS BU)](https://auto.huawei.com/cn/) and the [OPPO Research Institute](https://www.oppo.com/en/about/), working on trajectory prediction, multi-sensor calibration and LiDAR SLAM.

I earned my bachelor's degree at [Sichuan University](https://www.scu.edu.cn/) (**China 🇨🇳**) in 2019. I grew up in Qinghai, on the [Qinghai-Tibet Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_Plateau), and enjoy photography, travel and reading. 📷
